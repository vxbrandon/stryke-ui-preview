STRYKE — the flow, as built

Recording → shot cut → upload → ball solve → result on the phone, plus the optional hero and body-mesh stages. Written 2026-09-18 from the code on branch vxbrandon/dev-2026-09-17-2, not from the 28 August specs (those describe a larger system that was not built this way; see the last section). Every stage names the file that owns it.

1. One shot, end to end

① RecordiPhone, native Swift
AVFoundation session at 1080p60, rolling ring of 8 s segments (5 kept), locked exposure, 1 ms shutter cap.
modules/stryke-capture/ios/CaptureController.swift, SegmentRing.swift, CaptureTuning.swift
② Detect the kickaudio impulse only
Short-term energy on the mic: onset when one 1024-sample hop jumps > 6× the rolling median and clears −40 dBFS; 800 ms refractory. Confidence capped at 0.85. Nothing visual confirms it, so claps and net hits also trigger. Manual fallback: the 짧은 클립 저장 button.
ShotDetector.swift
③ Cut the clipno re-encode
Passthrough cut of [T−1.0 s, T+2.5 s] from the ring on 0.5 s IDR boundaries → shot_<n>_<UUID>.mov in Application Support, plus a sidecar JSON (focal length, per-frame PTS, gravity/attitude track, device record).
ClipCutter.swift, ClipSidecar.swift, SidecarTracks.swift
④ Save locallySQLite on the phone
Shot row inserted before any network call; the Stop button never waits on the server.
src/features/training/real-pipeline.ts, store-core.ts, db/schema.ts
⑤ Uploadone multipart POST
Register session → POST /v1/training/sessions/{id}/shots with the clip as form field clip (expo-file-system native task, byte progress since 2026-09-18) → POST /v1/analysis/jobs → complete session. The sidecar is not uploaded. Automatic sweeps re-register but never spend data; clips move only on 지금 보내기 (5 per tap).
real-pipeline.ts, api.ts, sweep.ts, api/client.ts
⑥ Jobbe-api, FastAPI
Job row (state, attempt, failure_class, refusal_reasons). Dispatched in-process to the vision service; the app polls GET /v1/analysis/jobs/{id}.
apps/be-api/…/analysis/service.py, ports/vision.py
⑦ Ball solvebe-vision → stryke-pipeline
The worker on :8015 runs pipeline.ball.analyze_video in-process. Two entry strategies, then a ballistic fit with drag and Magnus, then a band.
apps/be-vision/…/ball_lane.py → stryke-pipeline/pipeline/ball.py
⑧ Resultband, placement, arc
be-api turns the flight into a speed band (never a point unless ≥ 20 detections and every claim gate passes), a goal-mouth cell, and a trajectory table. The phone shows the band, the cell, the arc.
core/ball_result.py, core/confidence.py, core/placement.py, core/trajectory.py
⑨ Hero (automatic)after every solved flight
Offline render of the real trajectory with a generic CC0 body — not the user's mesh.
hero_lane.py → pipeline/heroshot.py
⑩ Body mesh (on demand)user presses 메시
Starts a GPU EC2 box, uploads the clip and mesh_run.py, runs GVHMR, exports a body sequence. Subject = the person who moves like a kicker (since 2026-09-18; it was the longest track, which picked bystanders).
mesh_lane.py, gpu_box.py, remote/mesh_run.py

2. Inside the ball solve (stage ⑦)

This is the part that refused all four field clips. ball.py is 6,500 lines; the order it tries things is what matters.

Sub-stageWhat it doesRefuses with
Read + stabiliseDecodes frames, flags duplicated frames (variable-rate phones), estimates camera shake and compensates.timebase_untrusted, retimed_suspected (duplicate ratio ≥ 0.5)
Entry A — rest ballFinds a stationary white circle (Hough) that later departs; seeds the track from it.rest_ball_not_located, rest_ball_no_departing_track
Entry B — moving ballOnly if A refuses. Blob tracklets → strike candidates (slow then fast) → re-track with the fused ensemble → must be ballistic in pixels, radius-consistent, ≥ 12 post-contact detections, and prove it is a ball (white disc confirmed in both halves of the flight).moving_ball_no_strike_detected, …flight_not_ballistic, …insufficient_ball_identity_evidence
TrackFrame-differenced blobs + Hough locks, DET_CONF ≥ 0.3, ≤ 6 consecutive misses, min radius 3 px.< 8 detections → too_few_detections (the field clips)
FitNine-parameter ballistic fit (launch speed, elevation, azimuth ±60°, g_eff, two Magnus terms, camera height/tilt, launch instant), Reynolds-dependent drag, basin search over camera geometry.rms > 30 px, ≥ 3 parameters on a bound (solver_railed), anchor disagreement > 35 %
ScaleMetres come from the ball diameter and, when the goal is detected, the goal width. Sidecar focal length replaces the diagonal heuristic when present (it is not uploaded today).8–19 detections → band only, no point speed
What the field clips hit: phone ~30 m back at night, ball < 10 px, teammate 3 m from the lens, two other balls on the pitch. Entry A found 2–4 white circles and none departed; Entry B found a moving candidate that could not be confirmed as a ball. Correct refusal. Report with frames: docs/reports/2026-09-18-field-clips/.

3. What the phone can show (states and refusals)

Shot statusMeaning
pending_upload → uploading → uploaded → queued → analysingThe clip's journey. Drawn as 업로드 대기 / 확인 중.
readyA band exists. Point speed only when claim_point_speed is true (≥ 20 detections and no forcing reason).
refusedThe pipeline looked and declined. Reason shown with a fix line from copy.ts refusalReasons.
failedInfrastructure, not measurement (upload rejected, job crashed). Retryable.

Refusal reasons the backend can actually emit today: too_few_detections low_speed_confidence retimed_suspected timebase_untrusted solver_railed. Defined in the contract and translated in the app but never produced: no_metric_reference contact_occluded contact_disagreed ball_too_small camera_motion_exceeds_tolerance no_kicker_detected kicker_not_fully_in_frame multiple_balls. Mesh and hero have their own state machines (MeshState/MeshReason, HeroState/HeroReason in contracts/…/vision.py).

5. What is not built (the specs say it is)

  • No visual confirmation of the kick, no fusion with audio, no speed-of-sound correction. 02 §3.1
  • No capture-time distance or framing check: goal and body detectors are compiled off; only the tilt chip is live. A player at 30 m gets a green chip. 02 §2.4, 03 §5
  • No shot segmentation inside a clip: one clip is one shot. 03 §1.3 S1
  • No pose lane, no contact-from-kinematics, no Verified/Quick calibration backend, no field profiles. 03 §1.3 S3–S6, §3–§4
  • No TUS/chunked/background upload; no SSE; no job reaper or retry budget. 02 §6, 04 §7
  • The sidecar is written but never uploaded, so the solver still guesses the focal length. SIDECAR-CONTRACT §7
  • Rankings serve only seeded test accounts; the route the spec forbade is the one that exists. 04 §8.8
